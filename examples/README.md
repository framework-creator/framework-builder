# Framework Examples

These frameworks were built using the Framework Builder methodology in SKILL.md. Each demonstrates the five-layer DNA architecture applied to a different domain.

## Available Examples

**[Client Onboarding](example-client-onboarding-framework.md)**
Service businesses, professional services, agencies. How to onboard clients in a way that prevents churn and builds long-term retention. [Guide](https://howtoframework.com/client-onboarding-framework) | [Page](https://whereframeworks.com/frameworks/client-onboarding)

**[Cross-Domain Pattern Transfer](example-toyota-production-system-framework.md)**
How to find proven solutions in other industries and transfer the mechanism to your problem. Illustrated through Toyota's 1896 manufacturing breakthrough and modern AI agent architecture.

**[Constraint-Driven Innovation](example-constraint-driven-innovation-framework.md)**
Converting limitations into competitive advantages. Validated across 145 studies. Applies to individuals, teams, and organizations operating under resource, time, or distribution constraints.

**[Delegation Brief](example-delegation-brief-framework.md)**
How to hand work to another person or an AI so it comes back right the first time. The framework the main README recommends starting with.

**[Failure-Mode Completeness](example-failure-mode-completeness-framework.md)**
Framework builders, operators, AI workflow designers. How to find the ways a process fails, including the ones that make no noise, with a stopping rule and a blind check.

**[Pricing](example-pricing-framework.md)**
How to set prices from the value delivered instead of costs or competitor numbers: segment routing, tiers and anchoring, and the metrics that show whether it works. [Guide](https://howtoframework.com/pricing-framework) | [Page](https://whereframeworks.com/frameworks/pricing)

**[Decision-Making](example-decision-making-framework.md)**
How to route decisions to the right process, collect written judgments first and name who decides. [Guide](https://howtoframework.com/decision-making-framework) | [Page](https://whereframeworks.com/frameworks/decision-making)

**[Business Strategy](example-business-strategy-framework.md)**
How to turn strategy into a filter, a kill list and a review rhythm a whole team can use. [Guide](https://howtoframework.com/business-strategy-framework) | [Page](https://whereframeworks.com/frameworks/business-strategy)

**[Competitive Analysis](example-competitive-analysis-framework.md)**
How to compete with larger rivals by mapping their constraints and tracking positioning. [Guide](https://howtoframework.com/competitive-analysis-framework) | [Page](https://whereframeworks.com/frameworks/competitive-analysis)

**[Problem-Solving](example-problem-solving-framework.md)**
How to diagnose the real cause before fixing it and keep a pattern library. [Guide](https://howtoframework.com/problem-solving-framework) | [Page](https://whereframeworks.com/frameworks/problem-solving)

**[Risk Management](example-risk-management-framework.md)**
How to cap dependencies, score risk by velocity and give every risk an owner. [Guide](https://howtoframework.com/risk-management-framework) | [Page](https://whereframeworks.com/frameworks/risk-management)

**[Project Management](example-project-management-framework.md)**
How to match the method to the project with an intake, three categories and retros that change something. [Guide](https://howtoframework.com/project-management-framework) | [Page](https://whereframeworks.com/frameworks/project-management)

**[Quality Control](example-quality-control-framework.md)**
How to put quality with the makers using control points, definitions of done and root-cause reviews. [Guide](https://howtoframework.com/quality-control-framework) | [Page](https://whereframeworks.com/frameworks/quality-control)

**[Sales](example-sales-framework.md)**
How to run B2B sales with discovery before demo, staged routing and mutual action plans. [Guide](https://howtoframework.com/sales-framework) | [Page](https://whereframeworks.com/frameworks/sales)

**[Content Strategy](example-content-strategy-framework.md)**
How to build topic clusters around real buyer objections and get sales to distribute them. [Guide](https://howtoframework.com/content-strategy-framework) | [Page](https://whereframeworks.com/frameworks/content-strategy)

**[Marketing Campaign](example-marketing-campaign-framework.md)**
How to route campaigns by urgency, prove one channel first and learn from every run. [Guide](https://howtoframework.com/marketing-campaign-framework) | [Page](https://whereframeworks.com/frameworks/marketing-campaign)

**[AI Prompt](example-ai-prompt-framework.md)**
How to turn prompting into a team system with context-first templates and a scored rubric. [Guide](https://howtoframework.com/ai-prompt-framework) | [Page](https://whereframeworks.com/frameworks/ai-prompt)

**[Film & Audio](film-audio/)**
Audio Visual Production Intelligence for sound engineers, film professionals, and post-production teams. Includes machine-readable JSON companions.
